Shop Capture

Privacy policy

Effective November 16, 2026. Updated November 25, 2026 to cover the beta sign-up form.

Shop Capture does not collect anything. Your notes and photos stay on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud Drive, in your own iCloud account. We never see them.

What the app stores, and where

The notes you write and the photos you take in Shop Capture are saved as ordinary files: one Markdown text file and its JPEG photos per note.

  • If iCloud Drive is turned on for the app, the files are kept in the app's own folder in your iCloud Drive. Apple syncs that folder between your devices under your Apple Account. That syncing is done by iOS and is covered by Apple's privacy policy, not this one.
  • If iCloud Drive is off or unavailable, the files are kept on your iPhone only.

The app also keeps a few small settings on your iPhone: which note was open last, any text typed but not yet added, which hand you hold the phone in, and your screen settings.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It contains no code that sends data over the internet. North Bench Labs has no server that receives anything from the app, and no way to read your notes or photos.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera. Used only when you open the camera screen, to take a photo into the current note. Photos are saved into that note's folder. They are not added to your photo library.
  • iCloud Drive. The app can read and write its own folder only. It cannot see anything else in your iCloud Drive.

The app never asks for the microphone, your photo library, your location or your contacts.

Dictation and keyboards

Shop Capture does no speech recognition of its own. If you dictate, that is done by the keyboard you have chosen, whether Apple's or a third-party one such as Wispr Flow. What that keyboard does with your voice or text is covered by its maker's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete a note, or a single photo, inside the app. Deleted items normally go to Recently Deleted in the Files app, where you can recover or permanently remove them. Because notes are plain files, you can also delete them directly in the Files app or in Finder on a Mac. Removing the app removes the settings it kept on your iPhone.

Beta sign-up

The app collects nothing, but the beta sign-up form on this website does. If you fill it in, North Bench Labs receives what you enter: your email address and your answers to a few questions about your work.

  • What it is used for. Sending you the TestFlight invitation and news about the beta, and, only if you said yes on the form, getting in touch to ask how the app is working for you.
  • Who else sees it. No one. It is not sold or shared. The form is run by Google Forms, so your answers are stored in Google's systems under Google's privacy policy. If you join the beta, Apple's TestFlight handles the invitation under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Removing it. Email hello@northbenchlabs.net and your entry will be deleted.

This website

This site uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log technical details such as visitors' IP addresses, as described in GitHub's own privacy statement.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects any of this, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.

Questions
